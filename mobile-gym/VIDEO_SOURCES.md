# Video Sources

The app uses short muted clips for every planned exercise. The machine clips use public-domain CDC footage where available. The remaining warm-up, core, cable, and posture clips are locally generated form loops made for this plan, so there is no third-party media to track for those files.

## Public-Domain CDC Clips

These source videos are public-domain works of the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, part of the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services.

- `leg-press.mp4`: trimmed from CDC `Seated_Leg_Press_and_Tip_Ipod-Lg.mp4`
- `chest-press.mp4`: trimmed from CDC `Chest_press_Ipod-Lg.mp4`
- `row.mp4`: trimmed from CDC `Row_machine_Ipod-Lg.mp4`
- `cable-row.mp4`: same source as `row.mp4`
- `leg-curl.mp4`: trimmed from CDC `Leg_Curl_Ipod-Lg.mp4`
