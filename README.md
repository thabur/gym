# Gym Routine Dashboard

This is the first 4-week gym block. The goal is to rebuild routine, lose weight slowly, and train around the lumbar disc history and current neck sensitivity.

## Current Setup

- Gym: Wellness Club, Strada Chiristigiilor 46, Obor
- Travel: walk by default, about 20 minutes each way
- Gym time cap: 40 minutes inside
- Schedule: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday
- Training style: machines + cables
- Start date: Monday, 2026-05-11

## The Rule

Show up, do the planned session, leave with energy still in the tank.

Do not chase soreness. Do not train to failure. Do not improvise heavy free-weight versions in month 1.

## Safety Checks

Before every session, rate:

- Lumbar pain: 0-10
- Neck pain: 0-10
- Radiation into arm or leg: yes/no

If pain is above 3/10 or there is radiating pain, use the fallback session from the program.

Urgent medical check if symptoms include new/worsening weakness, numbness, bladder/bowel changes, saddle numbness, or severe unusual headache.

## Success Criteria

By Saturday, 2026-06-06:

- Complete at least 14 of 16 sessions.
- No repeated next-morning pain flare.
- Gym feels like a normal appointment, not a heroic project.
