# Session Cards

Use this on your phone at the gym.

## Before Every Session

- Lumbar pain 0-10:
- Neck pain 0-10:
- Radiation into arm/leg: yes/no

If pain is above 3/10 or radiation is present, do the fallback session in [4-Week Gym Program](./gym-program-4-week.md).

## Monday - Strength A

1. Dead bug - 2 x 6/side - 30s
2. Bird dog - 2 x 6/side - 30s
3. Leg press - 2 x 10 - 90s
4. Seated row or chest-supported row - 3 x 10 - 75s
5. Machine chest press - 2 x 10 - 75s
6. Seated or lying leg curl - 2 x 12 - 60s
7. Cable Pallof press - 2 x 10/side - 45s

## Tuesday - Easy Posture + Core

1. Chin tucks, gentle - 2 x 6 - 30s
2. Wall slides or scapular retractions - 2 x 8 - 30s
3. Neutral-grip lat pulldown - 2 x 10 - 60s
4. Cable face pull, light - 2 x 12 - 60s
5. Hip abduction machine - 2 x 12 - 60s
6. Cable Pallof press - 2 x 10/side - 45s
7. Easy bike or treadmill walk - 8 min

## Thursday - Strength B

1. Side plank from knees - 2 x 15-20s/side - 30s
2. Dead bug - 1 x 6/side - 30s
3. Hip thrust or glute bridge machine - 3 x 10 - 75s
4. Neutral-grip lat pulldown - 3 x 10 - 75s
5. Machine chest press - 2 x 10 - 75s
6. Leg press, lighter than Monday - 2 x 12 - 90s
7. Cable row, light/moderate - 2 x 12 - 60s

## Saturday - Easy Full-Body Circuit

1. Leg press - 2 x 10 - 45-60s
2. Seated row - 2 x 10 - 45-60s
3. Machine chest press - 2 x 10 - 45-60s
4. Seated or lying leg curl - 2 x 12 - 45-60s
5. Cable face pull - 2 x 12 - 45-60s
6. Cable Pallof press - 2 x 8/side - 45s
7. Easy bike or treadmill walk - 5-8 min
